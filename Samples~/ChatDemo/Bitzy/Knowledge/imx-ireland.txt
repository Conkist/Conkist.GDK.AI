---

## What is ACM IMX?

ACM IMX is the premier academic and professional venue for research on interactive media experiences. It brings together the absolute best minds in:
*   **Interactive Entertainment** and immersive media design.
*   **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and natural language processing.
*   **Virtual Characters, AI, and UX design**.

We are going to present our work, share our findings on SmartAgents, and demonstrate how games and AI can combine to create deeply personal, growth-oriented human experiences.
